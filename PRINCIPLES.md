# AI-Native Product Principles

What "AI-native" means for OSWP --- not generic AI buzzwords, but concrete patterns couples will experience.

## 1. AI Generates, Couples Curate

Every major content creation moment starts with AI-generated options that couples refine.

- **Wedding websites**: AI drafts the "Our Story" section, suggests layout and color schemes based on couple preferences, generates RSVP wording
- **Guest communications**: AI writes save-the-date text, invitation wording, thank-you notes --- couples edit and personalize
- **Seating arrangements**: AI proposes table assignments based on guest relationships, dietary needs, and social dynamics

The couple is always the editor, never the blank-page author.

## 2. Smart Defaults Over Empty Forms

No field should start blank when AI can make a reasonable first guess.

- Timeline builder pre-fills a realistic wedding day schedule based on venue type, guest count, and season
- Budget tracker suggests allocations based on location, guest count, and priorities the couple sets
- Vendor search ranks results by fit, not just proximity --- factoring in style, budget, and availability

## 3. Proactive Nudges, Not Passive Dashboards

The platform reaches out when something needs attention.

- "12 guests haven't RSVPd and your deadline is in 5 days --- want to send a follow-up?"
- "Based on your timeline, you should book a photographer in the next 2 weeks. Here are 3 matches."
- "Your seating chart has 3 guests with dietary restrictions at tables without suitable meal options."

Nudges are actionable (one-tap to resolve) and respect frequency limits.

## 4. Personalization That Compounds

The more a couple uses the platform, the better it gets.

- Style preferences learned from website customization choices inform vendor recommendations
- Guest interaction patterns (who RSVPs fast, who needs reminders) improve communication timing
- Budget decisions in one category inform suggestions in others

## 5. AI Handles the Tedious, Couples Keep the Joy

Automate the logistics so couples can focus on the celebration.

- Auto-categorize RSVPs, dietary restrictions, and plus-one responses from free-text replies
- Generate vendor comparison summaries from reviews and pricing data
- Draft day-of timeline updates when things change (venue switch, weather contingency)
- Sync changes across all affected systems (guest list change updates seating, meal counts, and place cards)

## Anti-Patterns We Avoid

- **AI for AI's sake**: No chatbot that just wraps ChatGPT. Every AI feature solves a specific wedding planning pain point.
- **Black box decisions**: Always show the couple why AI suggested something. "We recommended this photographer because they match your bohemian style and are in budget."
- **Over-automation**: Never send a message, confirm a vendor, or make a payment without explicit couple approval.
- **Generic content**: AI-generated text should feel personal to this couple, not like a template with names swapped in.
